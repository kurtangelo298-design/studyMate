🔒 Privacy and Account Protection Policy

StudyMate PH
Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

StudyMate PH is an all-in-one academic productivity application designed to assist students in organizing their class schedules, assignments, project deadlines, and study materials in a single, accessible platform. We are committed to protecting the privacy, personal information, and account security of every user. This policy outlines what information we collect, how we use and safeguard it, and your rights as a user.

2. INFORMATION COLLECTED

We adhere to a minimal data collection principle. We only request information that is strictly necessary to create and maintain your account:

Information Purpose
Full Name To identify you within the application and personalize your experience
Username To serve as your unique identifier for secure account access
Password To authenticate and protect your account from unauthorized access
Security Question & Answer To verify your identity and facilitate account recovery if you forget your password

We do not collect location data, contact lists, device identifiers, photographs, or any additional personal information without your explicit consent.

3. DATA PROTECTION AND USAGE

  • Your information is used solely to provide, maintain, and improve the services offered by StudyMate PH
  • Your password is protected and never visible to any party, including the developers
  • Your security answer is stored privately and used only by you for account recovery
  • We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the application
  • Your schedules, tasks, notes, and preferences are stored locally on your device and remain under your control

4. ACCOUNT SECURITY

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials
  • You may update your account information or change your password at any time
  • You may delete your account and all associated data permanently whenever you wish
  • If you change devices or clear your browser data, locally stored information may not be transferred — we recommend maintaining personal backups of important records

7. YOUR RIGHTS

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate or updated information
